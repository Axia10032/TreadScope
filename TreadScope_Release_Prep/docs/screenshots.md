# TreadScope v1.1.38 screenshots

These captures illustrate the interface; their settings and values are examples for the displayed projects.

## Acquisition

![Five-trial simulated sequence and its selected trial](../assets/screenshots/v1.1.38/acquisition.png)

Recording spans appear under the live views. In group review, a click on a shaded span selects that trial; the selection is shown in green.

## Stillness & Automation

![Automation sequence timing, preview, timeout and trigger criteria](../assets/screenshots/v1.1.38/automation-rules.png)

Configure sequence timing and preview a trigger rule on the left. Enable trigger criteria and choose any or all on the right.

## Replay

![Single-recording latency review with event labels and confirmation interval](../assets/screenshots/v1.1.38/replay-latency.png)

The latency review separates recorded stimulus ON, estimated arrival, event onset and confirmation. This capture shows the ordinary single-recording progress bar.

## Analysis Rules

![Four response criteria and a shared timing illustration](../assets/screenshots/v1.1.38/analysis-rules.png)

Configure response criteria on the left and inspect their shared measurement windows on the right.

## Analysis

![Analysis toolbar, condition distributions and trial table](../assets/screenshots/v1.1.38/analysis.png)

Calculate and export from the toolbar. Compare groups side by side at each airspeed; narrow the dataset with project and trial filters.

## Quality Review Rules

![Recording checks and optional numeric bounds above a flagged coverage bar](../assets/screenshots/v1.1.38/quality-review-rules.png)

Choose recording checks and optional numeric bounds. The preview reports the number of recordings flagged and counts by reason; one recording can have multiple reasons.

## Project Data

![Project catalog with review and confirmed states next to a waveform inspector](../assets/screenshots/v1.1.38/project-data.png)

The inspector separates waveforms and derived results, metadata, and history. Confirmed review is labeled in green; pending review is labeled in yellow.

## Explorer

![Explorer dashboard showing eight movement, response and progression views](../assets/screenshots/v1.1.38/explorer.png)

Use the coordinated views to compare stimulus-aligned patterns, trial progression and distributions.
